the first letter

My dear reader, I have always thought that the most interesting people are the ones still asking "what if" long after they were meant to stop. I am one of those. Give me an unfamiliar problem and my first instinct is not caution, it is curiosity — a rather immediate sense of I should like to find out how this works, and very soon after, I think I can do it.

I try to carry that with good manners and a stubborn kind of optimism, because I have found that both make the finding-out much pleasanter, for me and for whoever is finding out alongside me.

My road here was not perfectly straight, and I do not mind saying so. After school I gave a year to the Joint Entrance Examination — not the year I had first planned for, but one that taught me patience is a skill like any other, and that a second attempt is not a failed first one. It closed with a 90.2 percentile, and by way of it, a seat in the B.Tech Computer Science programme at the University of Lucknow, where I am currently reading.

Smart India Hackathon 2026 — Internal Qualifier

GeoLayer 3D

Team "THE-DEBUGGERS" · ranked 6th of 124 · top 54 shortlisted

A 3D ULPIN generation & vertical property mapping system — my team's attempt at Problem Statement 26011, exploring how land and property might be represented in three dimensions for multi-level spaces, rather than flattened onto a single plane the way land records usually are.

We built it under real hackathon pressure, leaning on AI-assisted development to rapid-prototype the architecture and logic together as a team — which is its own kind of puffed-sleeves moment: a little chaotic in the middle, and rather satisfying by the end.
